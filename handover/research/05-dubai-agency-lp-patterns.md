## Recurring patterns an India-built page likely misses

1. **WhatsApp is the primary lead channel** — 7/8 pages have a wa.me/api link or floating icon, often above forms. UAE buyers chat before they fill forms.
2. **+971 number and a physical Dubai address with recognizable district** (Sheikh Zayed Road, Business Bay, Tecom, DIP, Deira) — 7/8. It's the local-legitimacy stamp.
3. **Budget-qualifying forms, sometimes in AED** — Digital Gravity's AED 5K–100K+ dropdown; Appinventiv's budget tiers + "2 mins" response promise. Displayed AED pricing is otherwise rare.
4. **Government/mega-brand logo walls** (Government of Dubai, Emaar, Dubai Mall) outweigh Google-review counts, which are mostly absent.
5. **Partner/award badges over testimonials**: Google Premier/HubSpot Diamond, MENA Search Awards, Clutch.
6. **"Get a Proposal/Quote" beats "free audit"** — free-audit framing was nearly absent; "Free Consultation" is the standard free offer.
7. **UAE-policy name-dropping** (Vision 2030, UAE PDPL, Smart Dubai) as differentiation on tech pages.
8. **Arabic toggle is rare (2/8)** — competitive white space, not table stakes.
